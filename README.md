Conducted exploratory data analysis on a retail sales database using SQL Server to examine customer demographics, product categories, sales performance, and purchasing behavior. Explored database structures and investigated key business metrics, including total revenue, quantities sold, average selling price, and order and customer counts. Analyzed customer distributions by country and gender, evaluated revenue across product categories and individual customers, and ranked top- and low-performing products based on sales. Used SQL queries, aggregate functions, joins, subqueries, and ranking functions to summarize data and identify patterns that support business understanding and further analysis.
